# Temporary issue list (not a report)

High-value experiment-methodology issues found in the current snapshot:

1. **Report significance gate ignores paired-panel attrition and single-seed
   dominance.** `build_report._sig_gate` checks n/raw p/Holm/|mean|>std but not
   `n_dropped` or leave-one-seed-out sign flips. `paired_row` and the P0W
   warmup path drop unpaired seeds before calling `exact_signflip`, then the
   surviving subset can be called significant. Mirror `reportable_claim`:
   expose LOO stability and panel attrition in `exact_signflip`, and refuse
   "significant" when any paired seed was dropped or one seed owns the sign.

2. **Tuned-vs-tuned pairing only pins top-level steps/tokens/data.** After
   per-arm tuning, `baseline_tune._relaxed_pair_reason` deliberately allows
   lr/wd/warmup to differ, but it does not verify that non-tuned structured
   hyperparameters (seq_len, batch_size, eval subset, model shape, precision)
   are equal when `hyperparams` is present. Add that gate so a tuned
   comparison cannot change the evaluation or architecture recipe.

3. **Scale-ladder audit does not check optimizer/augmentation identity and
   silently accepts a non-constant LR.** At scale, a width/depth effect can be
   an optimizer/LR-transfer effect. Compare the declared `train_knobs`
   descriptors across tiers (fail on a mismatch, warn when unverifiable) and
   warn whenever LR varies across tiers, naming the transfer policy.

4. **v10 Holm correction shrinks its declared family to finite p-values.**
   Contrasts with a non-finite/missing p are filtered out before Holm, making
   the remaining tests look better powered. Keep every declared contrast in
   the family with a dummy p=1 (the same fail-closed rule used elsewhere).
